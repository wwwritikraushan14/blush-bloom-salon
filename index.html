Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free, but booking on WhatsApp guarantees your slot.

Do you use products for sensitive skin?

Yes. Tell us about your skin when you book and we choose gentle, suitable products.

Can I book bridal services in advance?

Yes, bridal dates fill fast. Book 2–4 weeks ahead and we schedule a trial session too.

Is there any advance payment?

No advance for regular bookings. Bridal packages take a small advance to hold the date.
